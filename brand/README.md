**The idea:** a page whose folded corner is a sandpiper's beak. Notes, with a bird in them. The eye and two lines of
text are cut out of the page; the beak is the one coloured part.

## Colour

| | HEX | RGB | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ink** | `#16161A` | 22 22 26 | The page and the wordmark on light backgrounds; the app-icon tile |
| **Brick** | `#E4572E` | 228 87 46 | The beak, always |
| **Warm white** | `#F4F2EE` | 244 242 238 | The page and the wordmark on dark backgrounds |

Interface accent (buttons, links, focus): `#C2441D` with white text on light themes (5.1:1), `#F0825C` with
`#1F0D06` text on dark themes (7.2:1). Brick on white is 3.7:1: fine for the beak, too low for small text, which is
why the interface uses the deeper and lighter accents.

## Type

The wordmark is **Inter ExtraBold** (SIL Open Font License 1.1), tracked −3.5 % and converted to outlines: the logo
files need no font. Inter is also the interface typeface, so the logo and the app read as one.

## Clear space and size

- Keep clear space around the logo equal to **the folded corner** (the side of the cut the beak sits on) on every side.
- Minimum sizes: the horizontal logo **96 px** wide on screen (25 mm in print); the mark **16 px**. Below 32 px use
  the small cut or the favicon.

## Do

- Use the two-colour mark wherever colour is available; one colour (black, white or brick) where it isn't.
- Place it on white, light neutrals, ink or the dark app background.
- Keep the beak pointing right, towards the name.

## Don't

- **Don't put the two-colour mark on brick orange** (or any similar orange): the beak disappears and it reads as a
  plain document icon. On orange, use the white one-colour mark.
- Don't recolour the page or the beak, or add colours.
- Don't detach, enlarge or rotate the beak, or flip the mark.
- Don't fill in the eye or the lines of text; they are what make it a page and a bird.
- Don't set "Sandpiper" in another typeface next to the mark; use the wordmark files.
- Don't use the regular mark below 32 px; use the small cut.
